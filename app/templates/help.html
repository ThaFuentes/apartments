A normal day

Follow this. The sections under it are the same jobs, with every tap.

  1. 1. Sign in Username and password. If a code is asked, enter it.
  2. 2. Check the property at the top If it asks, tap Yes, I'm at that property. If the name is wrong, change it before you log anything.
  3. 3. Home Today's stops are first, then the make-ready units. Tap a unit, or tap Open make ready.
  4. 4. At the unit Tap the trade when that work is done. Take a photo if you need one. Call if a contractor has that trade.
  5. 5. When you stop More, then Today. Gas and receipts for the pump. Miles for the odometer.
  6. 6. A planned stop Today's plan, open the line, then I did this.

Sign in

  1. 1. The first screen Username and password. At least 8 characters. Sign out is at the top after you are in.
  2. 2. The very first owner The screen says Set up your owner account. Your name, a username, and a password. Email can be left blank. Later people are added from People.
  3. 3. Forgot password Under the sign-in button. The link goes to the password-reset email, which can be different from the login email and from the 2FA inbox.
  4. 4. A second code Use the authenticator app, or the code emailed to the 2FA inbox. Two-factor setup stays on its own page. Chat cannot turn it off.
  5. 5. The bottom bar Make ready, Properties, Home, and More. Chat stays closed until you tap Chat.
  6. 6. Install Home can offer Install. After that, the phone opens Apartments like an app. The icon can also jump to Make ready or Today's plan.

The property

  1. 1. The name at the top That is the property the boards follow. Make ready, upkeep, and inventory all use it. Change it before you log work at a different place.
  2. 2. Yes, I'm at When you arrive, tap Yes, I'm at and the property name. That tells the record you are there.
  3. 3. A new property On Home, open Add a site. Type the property name, the city, and the state, then Save site. If you do not see Add a site, this login is already tied to its properties.
  4. 4. New units Open the property. Tap Add units. Building, then the numbers, such as 1000-1020. That creates every unit in that range.
  5. 5. Find it later Properties lists them by state and city. Pin to top keeps one at the top of your list.

Make a unit ready

This is the screen while you are walking the building.

  1. 1. Open it Tap Make ready on the bottom bar, or Open make ready on Home.
  2. 2. Start a turn If the unit is not on the board, open Start a turn, pick the unit, and tap Start make ready.
  3. 3. Find the unit Units past their target date are listed first. A name on a trade is that one contractor. No name means maintenance still has it.
  4. 4. Tap the trade Tap it when that work is done. Tap to add puts that trade on the unit. Trash out, paint, floors, spray, and resurfacing stay separate.
  5. 5. Undo If you tapped the wrong trade, tap Undo. It sits above the bottom bar for a moment.
  6. 6. Send it back On a finished trade, open Why they need to go back. Write why, then Send back. The trade is open again.
  7. 7. Dates Open Dates. Move-out date is when they leave. Target ready is the day it should be done. Save each one.
  8. 8. Occupied or ready to rent Mark occupied when someone moves in. Mark ready to rent when the work is finished and nobody lives there. An occupied unit will not take make-ready changes until it is vacant. Clear rentable status takes Ready to rent back off.

Open one unit

  1. 1. Properties Tap Properties, then the property name.
  2. 2. Go to unit Type the number, such as 210, and tap Open.
  3. 3. Search is different Search unit records is for a note, an appliance, or a vendor. It does not jump to the unit. Use Go to unit for the number.
  4. 4. On a phone The filters start as Make ready and Occupied. More filters has the rest, including each building. Green needs make-ready work. Blue means someone lives there.
  5. 5. Who lives there Under the unit name: Make ready, Occupied, or Clear. Clear means no occupancy is set.
  6. 6. Move-out and rent Open Move-out date, pick the day, and save it. Mark ready to rent shows when the unit is in make ready. Clear rentable status takes that off.
  7. 7. The bar Photo, Call, Trades, and Note sit above the bottom bar.
  8. 8. Trades Tap a trade name to put it on this unit. Then call one person for that trade only.
  9. 9. A note Note opens Add something this unit needs. Pick the kind, type what it is, and save it on this unit.
  10. 10. Equipment Each washer, HVAC unit, and the rest is its own card further down. A service phone is a call button. How we do this is the instructions for that one piece. A reminder says how often, such as a filter every month. Done · reset this reminder starts the count again.

Call and check in

  1. 1. From the trade On Make ready, Call shows under a trade when that contractor has a phone number. It dials the phone.
  2. 2. One trade only Open Call one contractor. Pick one person and one trade, then Call to this unit. The other trades stay with maintenance or the person who already has them.
  3. 3. Save a name first If Call opens the contractor list, nobody is saved yet. More, then This property, then Contractors. Name, company, phone, usual work, and a note. Save contractor. No insurance tracking.
  4. 4. Check in On that person's card, Check a contractor in. Unit, property, time, estimated hours, and the one trade. Check in.
  5. 5. Check out The same card has check out. The make-ready board shows who is on site, and it flags a visit that runs past the estimate.

Take a photo

  1. 1. On the unit Tap Photo on the bar. The camera opens. The picture is saved on that unit. On Make ready the same button says Photo of this unit. Tap Save photo if it does not send by itself.
  2. 2. A nameplate If the photo shows a brand, model, or serial, open Chat. A card is waiting. Read it. Tap Save to file the equipment. Until you save, it is only a photo.
  3. 3. Work photos They sit on the unit under Work photos.

Upkeep

  1. 1. Open it More, then This property, then Upkeep. The count on the button is how many reminders are due.
  2. 2. Read the card The task, the equipment, the unit or the property, and how long until it is due.
  3. 3. You did it Tap Done · reset this reminder. It comes back after the interval you set. There is no calendar. The number is the list.
  4. 4. A pool or other property piece Open Add pool or property equipment. That piece belongs to the property, not one unit. Name it. Every N days adds the reminder.
  5. 5. The full list Equipment inventory, on the same menu, is everything filed at the property you picked at the top. Open the unit from a row.

Get there

  1. 1. Directions On a property, and on the property list, Directions opens the phone's maps when an address is saved.
  2. 2. The property map More, then This property, then Property map. Pick the property. That is the uploaded picture or PDF, so the team can see the site. It is not the street map. Replacing it is a new picture or PDF for that property only.
  3. 3. Driving view On More, tap I'm driving. Buttons get bigger and this menu hides while you are on the road. Turn driving view off in the same place when you park.

Plan, miles, and receipts

More, then Today.

  1. 1. Today's plan Open Today's plan. Still to do is what is open. Tap Make a plan.
  2. 2. The plan form Pick the day and the property. Starting mileage and ending mileage are optional. Leave them blank if you will enter the odometer later.
  3. 3. One card per job Unit number, then what was done. Unit 12 is not the same card as unit 26. Or type one job per line. Save this plan.
  4. 4. After the stop Tap the line. I did this closes it. Don't need it closes it as not needed. Leave open puts it back. I only finished some asks how many. Someone else finished it asks their name.
  5. 5. Miles Open Miles. When you stop, type the odometer and tap Save odometer. The first number is the start. The next number adds the miles between them.
  6. 6. Or type the miles Miles driven, a note such as Odessa to Midland, then Add miles. If an odometer already covers that day, those typed miles are not added a second time. The map does not guess the miles.
  7. 7. Gas and receipts Open Gas and receipts. Kind is Gas, Food, or Other. Type the amount. Merchant if you have it. For gas, the odometer. Check the amount, then File expense.

No signal

  1. 1. The board you already opened Make ready, Home, and a unit stay on the phone after you have opened them once. If the page says you are offline, tap Open make ready when you are back in range.
  2. 2. A trade or a photo Tap it anyway. It sits in Waiting to send and goes through when the signal is back. The same tap is not saved twice.
  3. 3. Gas, miles, and a receipt They wait on the phone too. When the signal is back, check the line and tap File this. Not now drops it. A typed chat note with no photo and no money still sends on its own.

Chat

  1. 1. Open it Tap Chat at the top. Say one thing, such as "I need a trash out" or "paint is done in 210".
  2. 2. Read the card Chat shows the change first. Nothing is written until you tap Save. Discard throws that card away. New starts a fresh chat. Buttons on the screens save directly and do not ask twice.
  3. 3. No AI key Chat still walks the work one question at a time. The first choices are Make ready, Vendor, Units, Equipment, and More. One trade at a time. "The team" or "maintenance" leaves the contractor blank.
  4. 4. Help from chat Say help and this page opens.

Office

More, then Office. Look, at the top, changes colors. Arrange changes a wide desk. A phone keeps the bottom bar. Neither one changes the records.

  1. 1. Reports Open Reports. This week, not saved yet, is the live week. Save weekly report covers the team. Save my week is only your work. An owner or admin can also Save company report. One person can be picked for that person's week.
  2. 2. After it is saved Tap the title. Send from that page when you mean to mail it. Mail has to be saved in Settings first. A boss with an email can get the company report. CSV and Download stay on the page. A boss login can read what was saved and does not get the save buttons.
  3. 3. Audit log Who changed what, and when, on the units you can see. A recent removal can be restored from the unit, under Recently removed records.

A boss can look and cannot change the record. The first login can have an email. Later ones can omit it.

  7. 7. AI keys Optional. A blank key still leaves the buttons and search working. A key shows only its last 4 characters. 1st is tried first. Do not paste a key into chat.
